# MENAXVI remediation roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- preserve the imported corpus and establish version control;
- inventory files, dependencies, licenses, personas, and dangerous content;
- define product boundaries and release invariants.

## Phase 2 — Product and legal cleanup

- replace real-person impersonation with generic professional roles;
- trace, license, clean-room rewrite, quarantine, or remove imported material;
- consolidate overlapping squads and establish a canonical schema;
- remove broken runtime assumptions and undocumented dependencies.

## Phase 3 — Engineering quality

- validate manifests, links, modules, frontmatter, and workflow references;
- add automated tests and CI;
- isolate offensive-security content behind explicit authorization controls;
- create portable adapters rather than coupling the core to one runtime.

## Phase 4 — Productization

- guided company-context onboarding;
- measurable marketing use cases and acceptance criteria;
- role-based access, privacy controls, auditability, and support documentation;
- pilot deployments followed by evidence-based release approval.
