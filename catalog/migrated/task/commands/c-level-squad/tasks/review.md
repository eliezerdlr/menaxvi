# Review Executive Strategy

## Purpose

Run cross-functional quality and governance checks on an executive recommendation.

## Required inputs

- decision owner and deadline
- current evidence and constraints
- affected stakeholders and material risks

## Procedure

1. Check strategic alignment and evidence quality.
2. Review financial, operational, market, technology, security, AI, and people impacts.
3. Resolve conflicting assumptions and dependencies.
4. Confirm decision rights, controls, measures, and review cadence.

## Completion criteria

- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable
- alternatives, risks, and uncertainty are explicit
- recommendation has an owner, measure, deadline, and review trigger
- regulated or irreversible decisions identify required qualified human review

## Output

A concise decision brief and action register.
