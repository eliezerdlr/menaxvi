# Evaluate Technology Strategy

## Purpose

Assess a major platform, architecture, build-buy, or engineering investment decision.

## Required inputs

- decision owner and deadline
- current evidence and constraints
- affected stakeholders and material risks

## Procedure

1. Define business capabilities and non-functional requirements.
2. Compare options across fit, cost, security, resilience, talent, and exit risk.
3. Identify technical debt and migration implications.
4. Recommend a staged decision with validation gates.

## Completion criteria

- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable
- alternatives, risks, and uncertainty are explicit
- recommendation has an owner, measure, deadline, and review trigger
- regulated or irreversible decisions identify required qualified human review

## Output

A concise decision brief and action register.
