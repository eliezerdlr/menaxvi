# Plan Capital Raise

## Purpose

Prepare an evidence-based financing strategy without promising outcomes.

## Required inputs

- decision owner and deadline
- current evidence and constraints
- affected stakeholders and material risks

## Procedure

1. Define capital need, use of funds, runway, milestones, and alternatives.
2. Assess traction, market, team, economics, governance, and narrative readiness.
3. Model dilution and downside scenarios.
4. Create diligence, communication, and decision plans with qualified review.

## Completion criteria

- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable
- alternatives, risks, and uncertainty are explicit
- recommendation has an owner, measure, deadline, and review trigger
- regulated or irreversible decisions identify required qualified human review

## Output

A concise decision brief and action register.
