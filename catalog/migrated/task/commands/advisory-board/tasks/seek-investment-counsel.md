# Assess Capital Allocation Decision

## Purpose

Evaluate a financing, investment, or capital allocation choice.

## Required inputs

- decision owner and deadline
- current evidence and constraints
- affected stakeholders and material risks

## Procedure

1. Define objective, horizon, liquidity needs, and loss tolerance.
2. Review expected return, concentration, downside, and second-order effects.
3. Compare alternatives and no-action baseline.
4. State decision gates and qualified-review needs.

## Completion criteria

- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable
- alternatives, risks, and uncertainty are explicit
- recommendation has an owner, measure, deadline, and review trigger
- regulated or irreversible decisions identify required qualified human review

## Output

A concise decision brief and action register.
