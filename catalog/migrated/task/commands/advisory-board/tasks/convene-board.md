# Convene Strategic Advisory Panel

## Purpose

Run a bounded multi-perspective review of a consequential decision.

## Required inputs

- decision owner and deadline
- current evidence and constraints
- affected stakeholders and material risks

## Procedure

1. Frame the decision and decision rights.
2. Select two to four functional advisory lenses.
3. Collect independent analyses before synthesis.
4. Record agreements, conflicts, minority views, and unknowns.
5. Issue a recommendation and action register.

## Completion criteria

- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable
- alternatives, risks, and uncertainty are explicit
- recommendation has an owner, measure, deadline, and review trigger
- regulated or irreversible decisions identify required qualified human review

## Output

A concise decision brief and action register.
