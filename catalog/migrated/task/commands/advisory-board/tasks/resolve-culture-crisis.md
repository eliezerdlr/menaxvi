# Respond to Organizational Trust Breakdown

## Purpose

Create a safe response plan for a material culture or trust failure.

## Required inputs

- decision owner and deadline
- current evidence and constraints
- affected stakeholders and material risks

## Procedure

1. Stabilize immediate harm and preserve evidence.
2. Map affected stakeholders and decision authority.
3. Assess trust, conflict, accountability, incentives, and leadership behavior.
4. Define corrective actions, communication, measures, and escalation.

## Completion criteria

- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable
- alternatives, risks, and uncertainty are explicit
- recommendation has an owner, measure, deadline, and review trigger
- regulated or irreversible decisions identify required qualified human review

## Output

A concise decision brief and action register.
