# Task: Incident Response

## Preconditions

- An accountable owner approved the purpose, systems, evidence sources, and time window.
- Data handling, change authority, rollback contacts, and stop conditions are recorded.

## Procedure

Preserve evidence; classify severity; contain with approved reversible actions; eradicate root cause; recover safely; monitor; document lessons and residual risk.

Use read-only evidence first. Any change requires separate approval and a reversible plan. Stop on scope ambiguity, service risk, secret exposure, or unexpected personal data.

## Evidence output

Record authorization, scope, sources, timestamps, findings, severity rationale, remediation owner, validation result, unresolved questions, and residual risk.
