# Decision Support Quality Checklist

- [ ] Decision, owner, deadline, and scope are explicit.
- [ ] Claims are supported or labeled as assumptions.
- [ ] At least one viable alternative and the no-action baseline are considered.
- [ ] Material trade-offs, dissent, uncertainty, and second-order effects are visible.
- [ ] Recommendation traces to evidence and stated criteria.
- [ ] Actions have owners, dates, measures, dependencies, and review triggers.
- [ ] Qualified human review is identified where required.
- [ ] No named-person imitation, invented quotation, or personal authority claim appears.
