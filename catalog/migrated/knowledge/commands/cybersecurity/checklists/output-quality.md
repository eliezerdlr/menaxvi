# Defensive security output quality checklist

- [ ] System owner, purpose, scope, and approved evidence sources are recorded.
- [ ] Methods are read-only or use approved synthetic test fixtures.
- [ ] Findings distinguish observation, hypothesis, and confirmed risk.
- [ ] Evidence is reproducible, timestamped, and redacted.
- [ ] Severity reflects likelihood, business impact, exposure, and control strength.
- [ ] Each recommendation has an owner, priority, rollback path, and validation step.
- [ ] No offensive instructions, secrets, personal profiling, or out-of-scope targets are included.
- [ ] Uncertainty and residual risk are explicit.
